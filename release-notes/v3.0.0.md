# Roomba+ Card v3.0.0

> **Minimum:** integration **4.2** · Home Assistant **2025.5** (unchanged from
> 2.5.0). **Recommended: integration 4.2.20 / 4.3.0b8** (robot position,
> renamed rooms and Prime zones on the map; see below), at least 4.2.19 /
> 4.3.0b7. Verified against 4.2.19, 4.2.20 and 4.3.0b7.

2.5.0 put the card back in step with the integration. 3.0.0 changes how the
card finds its data, so it stops breaking every time the integration
renames something. It also gives Prime / V4 owners (Combo 205/405/505,
Max 705 and other robots set up in the cloud-only "Prime" mode) a full card
instead of a partial one.

It is a major version because three things work differently underneath:

- **How entities are found:** through Home Assistant's entity registry,
  instead of being guessed from their ids.
- **The Map tab's picture:** it is now the integration's room map, for both
  generations.
- **The Health tab** is now called **Care**.

Your YAML does not change. `default_tab: health` still opens the Care tab.

## Entities are found, not guessed

Up to 2.5 the card built every entity id itself, for example
`sensor.<robot>_readiness`. That broke in four ways:

- the user renamed an entity;
- the integration renamed one;
- Prime robots used other ids (`sensor.<robot>_prime_phase`);
- one id meant two different things (`image.<robot>_map` is the cleaning
  map on Classic but a raw diagnostic map on Prime).

Each of these broke a feature without any error.

3.0 now works like this:

1. It starts from the vacuum and looks up its device in Home Assistant's
   entity registry.
2. It takes every `roomba_plus` entity on that device.
3. It indexes them by the integration's `translation_key`, the stable
   internal name each entity has whatever its id or display name.

The results:

- A renamed entity is still found.
- Classic and Prime entities with the same role are found by the same key.
- When the same id means different things on the two generations, the
  registry tells them apart, so they are no longer confused.
- Ids are only built from strings in one place now: a documented fallback
  for installs where the registry is not available.

The card also now re-renders when any entity of the robot changes, instead
of only for a hand-maintained list of about 100 ids. That list had gaps
more than once (2.0.1, 2.5.0).

## Prime / V4: the whole card

- **Header:**
  - Station work while the robot charges: pads drying, pads washing, bin
    emptying. Prime reports this on the vacuum (`dock_activity`), not in
    the phase.
  - The cleaning mode (vacuuming, mopping, both), in the integration's
    words.
  - "No contact" when the cloud has lost the robot.
- **Errors you can understand:** the iRobot help catalogue's title and
  description ("Pad plate missing — Attach the pad plate to mop"). When the
  robot can still do part of the job, the card says which part ("Mopping
  not possible — vacuuming only").
- **Start check:** when the robot would refuse to start (pad plate fitted,
  robot lifted …), the header says why and which mode still works. Start
  stays enabled — the integration reports this but does not block the
  start, and neither does the card.
- **Rooms:**
  - From the Prime room select.
  - On a home with several maps, grouped by floor, with the robot's floor
    first and marked.
  - Rooms on other floors are kept: Prime's `clean_room` accepts them.
- **Map tab:** the Prime room map with rooms you can tap to select, and a
  floor switch (`select.*_prime_map`, "Follow the robot").
- **Care tab:**
  - Parts, each in its own unit (hours, washes, emptyings) as Home
    Assistant formats it. Parts that wear out get a bar for the share of
    life left. Maintenance counters (counted since the job was last done)
    show their value only.
  - The station: dock status, pad wash and pad dry status, **Empty bin**
    and **Wash pad** buttons, and the pad-drying switch.
- **Connectivity chip:** Prime's `connected` and connection-health sensors.

The parts rows have no "Mark as replaced" button. The integration's
`reset_*` actions record a replacement locally but do not reset the
robot's own part counter, and that counter is what these rows show.

## Map tab, rebuilt on the room map

The Map tab now uses `image.<robot>_rooms_map`, the integration's own room
picture for both generations, with its `calibration_points`. Everything
the card draws on it is placed with that one transform:

- **Rooms:** tap a room to select it for cleaning. This is the same
  selection as the header's **Rooms…** picker. The label sits at the
  room's centre, with its area and, on Classic, the accessibility tooltip.
- **Coverage:** the heatmap as an image layer over the rooms. Classic
  only, once the integration has matched the map to the robot's own
  position data.
- **Stuck spots:** where the robot got stuck repeatedly, from its own
  position data.
- **Doors and furniture**, Classic, once matched.
- **The robot's position**, Classic, once matched. The tracker publishes
  the raw pose and the map frame swaps its axes; the card converts between
  the two.

Each layer that has data gets a chip under the map to switch it off for
the session. The picture is cropped to the rooms, so a long, narrow home
is no longer a strip in a dark square. The map is capped at 70 % of the
screen height.

When the room map cannot carry the tab, the 2.5 coverage view stays. That
covers:

- no room map;
- no calibration;
- no rooms yet;
- the 980 without cloud.

On a Classic map not yet matched to the robot's position data, the
coverage view appears **below** the room map. The heatmap is in a
different frame then and cannot be placed on it, and nothing 2.5 showed is
lost.

## Wide cards: map beside the content

From 700 px of card width — a sections dashboard with a wide card, or a
panel view — the map gets its own column next to the header and tabs, and
leaves the tab bar. The card measures its own width, not the window's, and
switches back when it narrows. For sections dashboards the card declares a
full-width default and a half-width minimum.

## Care (was Health)

The tab is renamed to match what it is for: parts, the station and the
robot's health (tab icon 🧰). It contains everything it did before, plus
the Prime parts and station described above.

## Card diagnostics (⚙ tab)

**⚙ → Card diagnostics** lists, for every role the card uses (phase,
readiness, error, rooms, map, schedule, …), the entity it found and how it
found it:

- **key** — by translation key;
- **alias** — the Classic/Prime counterpart;
- **id fallback**;
- **device class**;
- **missing**.

It also shows the versions of the card, Home Assistant and the
integration, and the counts of rooms, favourites and parts.

**Copy for issue** puts the same information on the clipboard as
Markdown. It holds no states, no attributes and no locations — only entity
ids, versions and found/missing. If the browser blocks the clipboard
(plain http), a dialog shows the text to copy by hand. Please include it
when you open an issue.

## Visual editor

- The robot picker offers only `roomba_plus` vacuums.
- A new card in the card picker previews with your first `roomba_plus`
  robot instead of a placeholder.

## Fixed along the way

- **A resolved Classic error no longer comes back.** The vacuum's
  `error_code` attribute is the robot's raw error field, which the
  firmware does not reset when the robot docks after a failure. The header
  (new in 3.0) would have shown it on a docked robot, and the alert banner
  could stay up for days. The card now goes by the integration's `error`
  sensor, which applies exactly that suppression.
- **Recharging mid-mission** read "Paused" and offered **Resume** to a
  robot on a nearly empty battery. Both generations report a mid-mission
  recharge as `paused` (the cycle is still running). The header now shows
  "Recharging" with **Cancel mission**.
- **Classic error words:** the alert used a `label` attribute that
  `last_error_code` never had, so it always said "Error N". It now shows
  the integration's description and what to do.
- **Favourites:** both generations now read the vacuum's `favorites`
  attribute and start a favourite through `roomba_plus.run_favorite`. The
  card no longer searches buttons by id pattern, which missed renamed
  ones. When a favourite or station button is refused (robot offline,
  favourite not found), the reason shows in the header instead of nothing.
- **Re-rendering:**
  - The card now also re-renders when only an entity's attributes change,
    such as a room renamed in the app or the cleaning mode.
  - It skips the rebuild when the output is unchanged, so an open robot
    selector or keyboard focus is no longer reset by unrelated updates.
- **Coverage legend:** "High coverage" is now blue, matching the heatmap
  the integration paints.
- **Robot names** in the robot selector are escaped.
- **Config edits** with Home Assistant already loaded render right away
  instead of staying on "Loading…".
- **Without the entity registry** (fallback), two robots whose names share
  a prefix (`roomba`, `roomba_2`) no longer share entities, and the
  Classic `schedule_hold` switch is no longer taken for a Prime schedule.

## Contract guard v2 (CI)

A new CI job runs on every push and weekly:

- It clones the integration at `main` and at its newest `dev-*` beta
  branch.
- It checks that every translation key, action and action field the card
  uses still exists there (`scripts/contract_check.py`).
- It checks that every state slug the card compares against is still
  declared (`tests/slugs.test.ts`).

A rename on the integration side now turns CI red instead of quietly
switching a card feature off.

## Better with integration 4.2.20 / 4.3.0b8

Three map details use attributes the integration adds in 4.2.20 /
4.3.0b8. On older versions the card falls back as described:

- **Robot position:** the tracker's `map_x_mm`/`map_y_mm` place the robot
  in the maps' own frame. Without them the card swaps the raw `x_mm`/`y_mm`
  itself — the tracker reports them in the firmware's order, the maps with
  the axes swapped.
- **Rooms with an alias (Classic):** the room map now names rooms exactly
  like the room select, so a renamed room can be tapped on the map. Before
  4.2.20 such a room is drawn but only selectable through the
  **Rooms…** chips.
- **Prime zones:** the room select marks each entry as room or zone
  (`segment_type`). The card leaves zones out of the room choice on every
  floor. Before 4.2.20 it can tell only on the floor the room map shows;
  elsewhere a zone may appear, and `clean_room` then says it is a zone.
  A zone the room map draws among its rooms is shown but not selectable.

**Learned obstacles and keep-out zones** are not on the map yet. They come
from the iRobot map through a reader that currently returns nothing for
real installs; the integration is clarifying how the cloud describes them,
and the card adds them once that is settled.
